# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

delegated: Next.js 16 (App Router) + React 19 + TypeScript, GSAP + Lenis for motion, static prerender on Vercel. Pure TS domain logic (range simulator, French-amortization installment) tested with node:test.

## Users

Primary: recruiters and clients reviewing Daniel Yataco's front-end work. In-fiction: Lima drivers considering their first electric car, worried it will not reach the coast or the sierra.

## Product Purpose

HITO is a fictional EV dealership in Lima. The page must answer range anxiety with a working route simulator, show the monthly installment without a call, and book a test drive.

## Positioning

The only dealer page that tells you, for your car and your destination on the Panamericana, whether you arrive and where to charge.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Real photography from Pexels. Models belong to their makers; prices, installments, chargers and availability are demo data and labeled as such.
